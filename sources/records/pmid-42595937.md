## Extracted evidence
Basis: abstract only.
- **Design / setting:** systematic analysis of RCTs from the MED-ADHD repository (2026 version); sources include PubMed, BIOSIS Previews, CINAHL, Cochrane CENTRAL, EMBASE plus unpublished data from manufacturers/authors.
- **Population:** 171 RCTs screened, 62 assessed clinico-demographic factors; participant numbers not reported in abstract.
- **Factor:** clinico-demographic predictors/moderators (age, psychiatric comorbidities, sex, baseline ADHD symptom severity, ADHD presentation). **Comparator:** as in the source RCTs (not reported in abstract).
- **Outcomes:** efficacy and tolerability of ADHD medications.
- **Main results (abstract):** factors examined: age 56.4%, psychiatric comorbidities 53.2%, sex 46.8%, baseline symptom severity 27.4%, ADHD presentation 24.2% of the 62 RCTs. Most RCTs reported no significant findings; preliminary evidence of associations reported for age 17.7%, psychiatric comorbidities 17.7%, baseline severity 16.1%, sex 11.3%, ADHD presentation 4.8%. Risk of bias (RoB 2) high in 37% of RCTs. No effect sizes pooled in abstract.
- **Authors' stated limitations (abstract):** stringent sample selection and reliance on group-level analyses may limit power to detect predictors/moderators; evidence limited and inconsistent.
- **Funding / COI:** not reported in abstract.
