## Extracted evidence
Basis: abstract only.
- **Design / setting:** retrospective multicentre clinical cohort; country not reported in abstract.
- **Population:** 12,995 adults with ADHD (49% female); 62% initiated dexamphetamine (dAMP), 38% methylphenidate (MPH). Age distribution, diagnostic criteria: not reported in abstract.
- **Factor / comparator:** patient factors: DSM-based clinical presentation; objective indices of impulsivity and hyperactivity (QbTest per keywords). Treatment variable (not a patient factor, context only): stimulant class (dAMP vs MPH) and its interactions with the patient factors. Reference category for the presentation HR is not stated in the abstract.
- **Outcome:** time-to-transition (switch of stimulant class, move to non-stimulant, or complete discontinuation), censored at 12 months or last contact; multivariable mixed-effects Cox model with random intercept for clinicians.
- **Main results (abstract):** median time-to-transition 33.1 weeks (dAMP) vs 15.0 weeks (MPH); MPH vs dAMP HR = 1.45, 95% CI [1.36-1.55] for non-persistence; hyperactive/impulsive presentation HR = 1.14, p = 0.025; objective impulsivity HR = 1.05, p = 0.002; stimulant class x objective hyperactivity interaction p = 0.003 (HR = 1.09).
- **Authors' stated limitations:** not reported in abstract.
- **Funding / COI:** not reported in abstract.
