## Extracted evidence
Basis: abstract only. Fields the abstract does not give are marked.
- **Design / setting:** two datasets: a longitudinal developmental cohort and an independent cohort; country not reported in abstract. Whether treatment was randomized or naturalistic: not reported in abstract.
- **Population:** dataset 1: 6,687 typically developing scans and 1,114 ADHD scans; dataset 2: 355 typically developing and 477 ADHD participants (abstract). Diagnostic criteria, age range, sex: not reported in abstract.
- **Factor:** individual deviation of white-matter structural connectivity from normative age-related trajectories (baseline). **Comparator:** typically developing normative model; atomoxetine vs methylphenidate as treatment contexts.
- **Outcomes:** ADHD symptom trajectories; 12-week treatment response to atomoxetine and methylphenidate; follow-up imaging. Measurement scales: not reported in abstract. Follow-up: 2 years (symptom tracking), 12 weeks (treatment).
- **Main results (abstract, no numeric effect sizes given):** "Baseline deviations predicted 12-week treatment response to atomoxetine but not methylphenidate"; within-individual decreases in deviation over 2 years tracked symptom improvement; treatment-related reductions in deviation at follow-up imaging; deviations mediated age-related declines in ADHD symptoms. Effect sizes, CIs, p-values: not reported in abstract.
