# Research Question

> STATUS: CONFIRMED by the user for the test run (2026-11-02). Corrected from the original test prompt (see "Notes").

## Question
Which baseline clinical, biological, or other patient factors are associated with treatment response, loss of response to treatment, and clinical outcomes in patients with ADHD?

## Framework (flexible, not PICO-bound)
- **Population / system:** Human patients with a diagnosis of ADHD (children, adolescents, adults; record age group per study).
- **Exposure / factor of interest:** Baseline predictors or moderators of response (clinical, genetic, biomarker, neuroimaging, comorbidity, treatment history).
- **Intervention context:** Pharmacological (stimulant, non-stimulant) and non-pharmacological treatments.
- **Comparator (when relevant):** Placebo, active comparator, or treatment-as-usual.
- **Outcomes / endpoints:**
  - Treatment response (symptom change, responder rate)
  - Non-response or loss of response over time (e.g., tolerance, treatment failure, discontinuation for lack of efficacy)
  - Functional and long-term clinical outcomes
  - Adverse events leading to discontinuation (secondary)

## Study types
- Include: randomized controlled trials, prospective/retrospective cohort studies, case-control studies, systematic reviews and meta-analyses (including secondary analyses of trials).
- Exclude: case reports, editorials, conference abstracts only, preprints, animal-only studies, study protocols without results, conceptual/framework papers without study data.
- Narrative reviews: keep as candidates (flagged) for the reviewer to decide; use only to locate primary studies unless the reviewer rules otherwise.

## Date range
2022-01-01 to 2026-11-02 (publication date).

## Language
English.

## Inclusion criteria
1. Peer-reviewed, published in the date range.
2. Human participants with an ADHD diagnosis.
3. Reports an association between a patient factor and treatment response, loss of response, or clinical outcome. The factor must be a characteristic of the patient (clinical, genetic, biomarker, demographic, comorbidity, treatment history). Studies that only compare treatments, doses, schedules, or intervention types, with no analysis of a patient factor, do not meet this criterion. Study-level moderators in a meta-analysis or meta-regression (e.g., mean age, trial duration) are not patient factors for this purpose.

## Exclusion criteria
1. No ADHD-specific results (mixed neurodevelopmental samples without ADHD subgroup data).
2. No treatment-response or clinical-outcome measure.
3. Retracted papers.
4. Outside the date range, language, or study types above.

## Topic-specific constraints
- "Acquired resistance" is an oncology concept and does not apply to ADHD; it is replaced by "loss of response / treatment failure over time".
- Record whether each study is about children, adolescents, or adults.
